MON · DEC 21 · 10-MINUTE RECIPE

PEPPERMINT PROTEIN BITES.

CAPTION

10-min prep. A class starts every 10 minutes, and so does snack time. Peppermint Protein Bites: stir together 1 cup rolled oats, ½ cup nut butter, 2 tbsp honey, 1 scoop vanilla protein and 2 tbsp mini dark chocolate chips. Roll into 12 bites and dip the tops in crushed candy cane. Eat now or chill. Save this one for your next holiday party. 🍬 #Made4Busy
